# Sea Bottles

Hover over a drink from a Sailing sealed crate in your inventory or bank. The tooltip shows the bottle sprite, player safety, courier cargo safety, whether you can sample the drink again, and what happens when you sample it.

The drink data covers all 65 entries in the [OSRS Wiki sealed crate table](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Sealed_crate?oldid=15397474#Crate_locations). Cargo safety refers to courier task cargo. Dying on open waters can also cost you that cargo.
